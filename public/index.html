Puzzles

Pick a puzzle to play, draw your own with New puzzle, or import one with Load screenshot.

Features
  • Unlimited play: replay any puzzle as often as you like. Solved puzzles show a ✓ and your best time.
  • Board editor: paint the color regions of a puzzle yourself. Load a picture of the board behind the grid and trace over it.
  • Screenshot import: load a screenshot of a Queens board and the regions are detected for you to check in the editor.
  • Auto X's: placing a queen marks every cell it blocks. Turn it off with the Auto X button.
  • Undo: step back through your moves with Undo or Ctrl/⌘+Z.
  • Solutions: once solved, a puzzle remembers its solution so you can view it again.
  • Your library: rename (✎), reorder (drag ⠿) and delete (×) puzzles. Everything is saved in this browser.
